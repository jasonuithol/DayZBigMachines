# Changelog

## 0.1 (2026-11-24)

First release.

- Bulldozer: drivable crawler dozer with a working blade that shoves things.
- Excavator: crawler backhoe that drives along its tracks; slew, boom, stick and bucket.
- Tower crane: climb the mast's ladder, work slew, trolley and hook from the cab; latch, lift
  and set down items and crates.
- FAT livery and weathered paint (chips, grime, rust, muddy tracks).
- A configurable share of each map's bulldozer and digger wrecks and tower cranes are working
  machines instead (`$profile:BigMachines/BigMachinesSettings.json`, 25% each by default).
  Destroyed machines are replaced at the next restart. Chernarus, Livonia, Sakhal, Nasdara.
